# Changelog

A history of Ingoizer's World, newest first. Luca created the game, and the dates below are Pacific time. The version labels are only a way to read the story. The game itself does not show these numbers.

Every pull request from [#1](https://github.com/genemagg10/adventure-game/pull/1) through [#79](https://github.com/genemagg10/adventure-game/pull/79) was merged. None were closed without merging. Three changes were committed straight to main, and those are marked as direct commits.

## Highlights

- **February 26, 2026** — The world opens: a huge land, a rusty sword, Blue Gems, and the Black Knight at Ing Castle.
- **February 27–March 1, 2026** — A bow, the Lady of the Lake, sound, phone controls, armor, Merlin, and Earth, the fifth element.
- **March 15–April 11, 2026** — The Green Knight, four caves, health potions, and the Fountain of Youth.
- **July 13, 2026** — Dying sends you home to Green Meadow instead of ending the story.
- **July 23, 2026** — Ice opens a hidden room inside Ing Castle.
- **August 16, 2026** — The Worldtree, the Cloudlands, the Olympians, and animals you can tame with apples.
- **August 18, 2026** — The game's own address, luca.maggio.xyz, and a title screen painted like the realm.
- **August 20, 2026** — Maps that fill in as you walk, and a ladder in the corner down to the Maker's Hollow, where Luca signed the game.
- **August 23–25, 2026** — Saving the game, the Clubhouse, and the Worldtree Seed's real planting ground.
- **August 31, 2026** — Choose which Ingoizer sibling you play. The people you meet say that name.
- **September 13–20, 2026** — Sprinting on apple energy, and the Giant Snapping Turtle guarding the place the seed belongs.
- **October 5–7, 2026** — The Hall of Champions, gravestones, and a secret in the world where you can meet Luca.
- **October 10–13, 2026** — Day and night. Ice freezes the fountain and Crystal Lake. Fire can burn trees even at the edge of the map.
- **October 13, 2026** — Share with Luca: send a note from the title screen or the pause menu.

## v0.12 — Share with Luca

- **October 13, 2026** — [#79](https://github.com/genemagg10/adventure-game/pull/79) — Notes sent from Safari were getting stuck before they left the phone. They go through now.
- **October 13, 2026** — [#78](https://github.com/genemagg10/adventure-game/pull/78) — Friends can send Luca an idea, a frustration, a story, or something they found, from the title screen or the pause menu. The title menu leads with one big button, Continue or Begin Adventure, and tucks the rest into a tidy grid.

## v0.11 — Day, night, and a land that changes

- **October 13, 2026** — [direct commit](https://github.com/genemagg10/adventure-game/commit/228ef5ee1c2a93090b86393fcbf17d50b15f3bf4) — The live game caught up after a publish stumble, so the newest save message actually appeared at luca.maggio.xyz.
- **October 13, 2026** — [#77](https://github.com/genemagg10/adventure-game/pull/77) — Saving now says who the game was saved for, using the Hall name, as in "Game saved for Mara." If no name was set, it says Wayfarer.
- **October 13, 2026** — [#76](https://github.com/genemagg10/adventure-game/pull/76) — Fire can burn trees in the outer rows of the map, on every edge, and ice and earth can clear those edges too. You still cannot walk off the world.
- **October 13, 2026** — [#75](https://github.com/genemagg10/adventure-game/pull/75) — Ice lays a sheet over the Fountain of Youth and Crystal Lake, and the freeze thaws at dawn or after a while. Fire beside a tree leaves the same brown ash a fire arrow does. Space no longer opens the map.
- **October 10, 2026** — [#74](https://github.com/genemagg10/adventure-game/pull/74) — The surface and the Cloudlands pass through day, dusk, night, and dawn. Monsters are tougher at night and drop better treasure. Fire, water, ice, and earth can also clear matching ground, while lakes, the fountain, and other landmarks stay protected.

## v0.10 — Hall of Champions

- **October 7, 2026** — [#73](https://github.com/genemagg10/adventure-game/pull/73) — Strange keys sit in Relics. The Hall uses softer names for secret places, each cave is its own deed, and a gravestone marks the spot where you fell. A full set of deeds can wear a jade crown, and tapping a champion's name opens their list on a phone.
- **October 6, 2026** — [#72](https://github.com/genemagg10/adventure-game/pull/72) — Cave bats and Cloudlands eagles can be tamed with an apple. They follow, they fight, and they count toward the pack of five, including the Clubhouse.
- **October 6, 2026** — [#71](https://github.com/genemagg10/adventure-game/pull/71) — The Hall remembers helping the Lady of the Lake, helping Merlin, and beating Luca. That last deed is the secret meeting Luca left in the world. The prize from the fight stays off the public board.
- **October 6, 2026** — [#70](https://github.com/genemagg10/adventure-game/pull/70) — The Hall of Deeds became the Hall of Champions. Players are grouped by name and ranked by the weight of their deeds, and a crown marks the hardest one they have earned.
- **October 5, 2026** — [#69](https://github.com/genemagg10/adventure-game/pull/69) — After you choose a sibling, the game asks for a short name. First-time milestones from that run appear on a Hall you can open from the title screen or the pause menu. A hidden door also appeared beside the Maker's Hollow.

## v0.9 — Running, the turtle, and the pack

- **September 28, 2026** — [#68](https://github.com/genemagg10/adventure-game/pull/68) — The new pet fighting rules were undone the same morning. Companions were dying too quickly, so they went back to the way they fought before.
- **September 28, 2026** — [#67](https://github.com/genemagg10/adventure-game/pull/67) — For a short while, pets attacked only the enemy you had just hit, and they fought back when a monster hurt them. That experiment did not stay.
- **September 20, 2026** — [#66](https://github.com/genemagg10/adventure-game/pull/66) — A Giant Snapping Turtle guards the Fallow, south of Ing Castle, on the ground where the Worldtree Seed is meant to go. It is as tough as the Black Knight. Beating it drops Turtle Shell Armor, and no seed takes root anywhere until the turtle is gone.
- **September 14, 2026** — [#65](https://github.com/genemagg10/adventure-game/pull/65) — Eating an apple fills the whole energy bar, not just a quarter of it. A sprint still lasts about as long as before.
- **September 13, 2026** — [#64](https://github.com/genemagg10/adventure-game/pull/64) — Hold the sprint key, or the boot button on a phone, to run faster while an energy bar lasts. Apples are the only fuel. When the bar is empty, you walk until you eat.

- **August 23, 2026** — [#54](https://github.com/genemagg10/adventure-game/pull/54) — You can save. Three slots live in the pause menu, including on a phone, and the title screen offers Continue once a save exists. Monsters come back fresh. Your progress, and your tamed animals, stay.
